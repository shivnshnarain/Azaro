Profile
Azaro is a leading brand for long life, durable and comfortable office chairs for all business needs.
Mr. Manjul Agarwal, is the founder of Azaro brand which has become a highly recommended brand in the industry.
Azaro has been started since the year 1999 which itself is a proof of sustainability in the market.
Azaro intends to manufacture a wide range of comfortable office chairs that will match the high standards of market
and fulfill all the expectations of our customers.
At Azaro, we try to deliver style, comfort and durability; every chair is designed to suit all needs of our body. We
follow all quality measures along with latest technical equipments.
We have efficient and hardworking group of people who work very sincerely to meet our customer's requirements.
Our brand provides highest level of quality and very wide products range.
Our brand has been well known for its excellent quality of products, and now the brand is becoming very popular
because of our sincere dedication towards our customers.
