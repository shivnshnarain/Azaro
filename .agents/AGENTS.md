# Product Order Rule

Every NEW product requested must ALWAYS be appended at the END of the Products page (`ProductsCatalogue.tsx`).
Never insert a new product between existing products.
Never reorder previously created products.
Never place a new product near a reference product.

When a reference product is specified (e.g., "Make it like WESLEY 160"), it ONLY means to copy the card design, layout, styling, spacing, functionality, animations, and responsiveness. It DOES NOT mean to place the new product near that reference.

Always place the new product at the END of the catalogue.
